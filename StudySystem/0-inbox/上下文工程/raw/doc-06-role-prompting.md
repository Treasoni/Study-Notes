- **Author**: PromptingGuide.ai
- **Date**: 2024
- **Type**: guide

---

## Definition

Role prompting is a technique where you instruct an LLM on how to behave, its intent, and its identity. This is particularly useful for building conversational systems like customer service chatbots.

## Key Points

- You can explicitly tell the model how to behave through instructions
- The technique allows you to control the output style and technical depth of responses

## Example

Creating an "AI research assistant" persona with a technical and scientific tone, or instructing the same persona to give "easy to understand" answers for primary school students.

The same question about black holes gets different responses depending on the role instructions provided.

## Practical Applications

- Customer service chatbots
- Technical support assistants
- Educational tutors with varying complexity levels
- Industry-specific expert systems
